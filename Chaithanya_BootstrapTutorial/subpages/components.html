2. Roasting FAQ (Accordion)

Click accordion tabs to expand dynamic collapse animations.

First crack occurs around 196°C-205°C when internal steam pressure bursts bean walls, releasing sweetness. Second crack at 225°C signals dark carbonization and smoky oils.

Elevations above 1,600m experience cold nights that slow bean maturation, concentrating complex organic sugars, crisp malic acidity, and floral aromatics.

Freshly roasted beans emit CO₂ gas for 5-10 days. Extracting too early creates turbulent channeling and sour taste; resting yields sweet, silky crema.
